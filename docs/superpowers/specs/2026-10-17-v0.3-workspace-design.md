# crafted-herdr-ui v0.3: Dashboard home and a tabbed workspace (design addendum)

Date: 2026-10-17
Status: approved (the maintainer approved the Claude Design canvas "Crafted Herdr v0.3 — Workspace UX" on 2026-10-17: "Great, build this")
Extends: `2026-10-16-crafted-herdr-ui-design.md` (v1) and `2026-10-17-v0.2-in-app-terminal-design.md` (v0.2). Both still hold unless this addendum overrides them. Where they disagree, this addendum wins.

## 1. Intent

In v0.2 the terminal is a panel squeezed under (or beside) the Home list. The maintainer wants the terminal to **take over the middle of the window**. The Home list becomes a **Dashboard**: what needs you, a few live metrics, and the state of each workbox. The middle is **tabbed**, with the Dashboard pinned first and terminal tabs after it, **grouped per workbox**. Going back home is always one click or ⌘1. The two "Open here" / "Open in …" buttons become a single **"Open"**.

### What this overrides in v0.2
- **§2.1 "Built-in terminal panel"** (bottom or right, resizable, toggled with ⌃`) is replaced by the tabbed workspace in §2.1 below. Tabs, sessions, the detached state, the 16-tab cap, copy-only links, find and font size all carry over unchanged.
- **§2.3 "Split view (⌘\)"** now shows the Dashboard beside the active terminal, rather than moving the panel.
- **§2.2 Tabs:** ⌘1 is the Dashboard; terminal tabs move to ⌘2–⌘9.
- The **Inspector's** "Open here" / "Open in {Terminal}" pair, and the same pair on the needs-you cards, become the single Open button (§2.4).

## 2. Features

### 2.1 Tabbed workspace (the middle of the window)
- **Layout:** sidebar on the left; the workspace fills the rest. The workspace is a tab strip with the content of the active tab under it, at full size.
- **Home tab:**
  - It is first, always present and never closable. ⌘W on it does nothing.
  - It shows the current home view: the **Dashboard** (default), **All agents** (the v0.2 Home list with its Inspector, grouping and search), or a **Workbox** screen.
  - Its label is that view's name ("Dashboard", "All agents", or the workbox label), with a home icon.
  - ⌘1, the sidebar's Dashboard item and the palette's "Go to Dashboard" switch to it, showing the Dashboard.
- **Terminal tabs:**
  - They are the v0.2 terminal sessions, unchanged underneath: one attach client per tab, closing a tab only detaches, the detached state offers Reattach, at most 16 tabs.
  - The active tab fills the workspace. Inactive terminals stay mounted but hidden, so scrollback survives switching (v0.2 R17).
- **Grouped by workbox:**
  - In the strip, each workbox with open tabs gets a group label: health dot and workbox label, in mono. Its tabs follow it.
  - Groups appear in the sidebar's machine order. Within a group, tabs keep the order they were opened in.
- **Group folding:**
  - Clicking a group label folds that group into one chip: "{label} · N tabs", plus "N needs input" when any of its tabs needs input.
  - Activating a tab in a folded group, from the sidebar, palette, ⌘J or a notification, unfolds it.
  - Folding lasts only while the app runs; it isn't saved.
- **Tab content:** status dot plus the agent name (mono) and a close button.
  - A tab whose agent needs input, or finished unseen, also shows that status as a word chip ("Needs input", "Done, unseen"), with a light orange tint for needs-input.
  - Other states show the dot, with the status word in the tooltip and accessible name.
  - The Opening…, Detached and Error states keep their v0.2 wording, shown as a chip.
- **Overflow:** when the tabs don't fit, the strip scrolls sideways and the active tab is scrolled into view.
  - An "All tabs" menu button appears at the end of the strip. It lists every tab by workbox with its status, and shows a needs-input count when one applies.
- **Terminal context bar:** a bar above the active terminal shows:
  - the agent name, status pill, kind badge, and folder · workbox;
  - Find (⌘F);
  - **Open in {Terminal}**, the same attach handed to the user's terminal;
  - **Detach**, which closes the tab the way ⌘W does;
  - **Close pane…**, the v1 action with its confirmation.
  - A full-UI tab shows "herdr" and the workbox label, Find and Detach, and nothing else.
- **⌃` (Toggle Terminal):** switches between the Home tab and the most recently active terminal tab. With no terminal tab open, it shows the v0.2 hint "Open a session to start a terminal."
- **⌘\ (Dashboard beside terminal):** while a terminal tab is active, it shows the Dashboard in a column on the right of the terminal.
  - The column is resizable, 360 px minimum, and its width is saved.
  - Pressing it again hides the column. On the Home tab, ⌘\ does nothing.
  - The column uses the Dashboard's narrow, single-column layout. Its Open buttons open into the main side.

### 2.2 Dashboard (the new home)
Top to bottom, as on the canvas:
1. **Notices:** the existing ones (local herdr down, workbox needs attention, version banner), kept at the top as they are on Home today.
2. **Heading:**
   - "Dashboard" with a summary line "{W} workboxes · {A} agents · {T} terminal tabs open";
   - on the right, "Next needs-you ⌘J" and the primary "New session ⌘N".
3. **Needs you** ("{N} waiting"):
   - Agents needing input come first as cards, using `needsYou` order: blocked first, then oldest.
     - Each card shows the status pill with age, kind badge, name, and project · workbox.
     - It has the Open split button in the needs colour.
     - Cards on the Dashboard show **no terminal preview** in v0.3, because reading pane output would be a new herdr call per card. Clicking a card, or its Reply…, opens All agents with that row selected, where the live preview and reply already work.
   - "Done, unseen" agents follow as a compact list ("Finished, not looked at yet"), each row with an Open button.
   - With nobody waiting, the section shows "Nobody needs you right now."
4. **Right now:** five metric tiles. All come from data the app already has; there are no new herdr calls.
   - **Agents:** total, a bar split by status with a text legend ("2 need input · 3 done · 6 working · 3 idle").
   - **Blocked the longest:** age of the oldest blocked agent, and its name and workbox. "None" when no one is blocked.
   - **Workboxes online:** "{online} of {enabled}", plus the first non-online workbox's state ("gpubox reconnecting, retry in 8s").
   - **herdr response:**
     - It shows the median `durationMs` of herdr calls in the command log over the last 5 minutes, and the number of failed calls.
     - Terminal launches (entries with a `note`) don't count. With no calls, it shows "—".
     - It reads the in-memory command log on its own beat while the Dashboard is visible and the window isn't hidden. Each read is scheduled 5 s after the previous one ends.
   - **Terminal tabs:** "{n} of 16", with a per-workbox breakdown.
5. **Workboxes:** one card per workbox, showing:
   - health label (never colour-only) and the status-mix bar with a text legend;
   - freshness and the number of open tabs: an online workbox says "Up to date" (`last_ok_ms` only moves when the snapshot changes, so an age would grow while polls succeed); one that isn't online says when it was last reached ("Last updated 2m ago", from `last_ok_ms`);
   - one action:
     - **Open herdr:** full UI, following `open_target`, for an online workbox;
     - **Retry now:** the existing reconnect, for one that is reconnecting or needs attention;
     - nothing for a disabled workbox.
   - A workbox that isn't online shows its last snapshot dimmed, marked "as of {age} ago".
6. **Working now ({n}):** working agents, longest-running first, with project, workbox, "Working for {age}" and an Open button. A link "All agents ({n})" opens the All agents view.
- **Empty states:** with no agents at all, the Dashboard shows the existing Home empty and onboarding states where the Needs-you section would be.
- **Layout:** the Dashboard is fluid, max width 1180 px, with grids that wrap. The narrow layout (the ⌘\ column, or a narrow window) is one column.

### 2.3 Sidebar
- **Nav:**
  - **Dashboard** (⌘1);
  - **Needs you** with its count, which opens All agents filtered to needs-you, as today;
  - **All agents** with its count.
- **Workboxes section** (with "+" to add a workbox):
  - Each workbox row shows its health dot, label, health word and agent count, and opens that Workbox screen in the Home tab.
  - Under each workbox, its open terminal tabs are listed (terminal icon, mono name, status dot, status in the accessible name). Clicking one activates that tab.
- **Projects:** the first 3 projects, then "{n} more", which expands the list. A project opens All agents filtered to it.
- **Collapsed rail (⌘B):** Dashboard, needs-you, and the workbox icons with health. Nested terminals are hidden there.

### 2.4 The Open button
- One split button, labelled **"Open"**. The main part follows `open_target`, as Open does everywhere since v0.2.
- **The chevron menu:**
  - **Open here**;
  - **Open in {Terminal}**;
  - **Open full herdr UI** (that workbox's full herdr UI, following `open_target`);
  - a separator;
  - **Copy attach command**, which copies the same attach command the "Open in" path runs, and shows "Copied".
- **Already open:** if the row is open in a terminal tab, the main part reads **"Go to tab"**, with that tab's ⌘ number when it has one (⌘2–⌘9).
- **Where:**
  - needs-you cards, in the needs colour;
  - Dashboard lists and rows, the All agents Inspector and the Workbox screen, in the secondary style.
- **Keys:** the menu shows key hints only where those keys work.
- The menu is a real menu: arrow keys move through it, Escape closes it, and focus goes back to the chevron.

### 2.5 Keys and the native menu
- **Changes:**
  - **Dashboard ⌘1:** a new menu item.
  - **Tab 1–8:** these now take ⌘2–⌘9 and select terminal tabs in strip order. Folded groups count in order, and selecting a tab unfolds its group.
  - **Toggle Terminal ⌃`:** it now switches between Home and the last terminal tab (§2.1).
  - **⌘\:** its menu item is renamed "Dashboard Beside Terminal".
- **Unchanged:** ⌃Tab and ⌃⇧Tab cycle through all tabs, the Home tab included. ⌘W closes (detaches) the active terminal tab and does nothing on Home.
- The palette gains "Go to Dashboard" and "All agents".

### 2.6 Settings
- **Removed:** "Terminal placement" (bottom/right) and the panel size.
- **Saved:** whether the ⌘\ column is shown, and its width. Existing settings files with the old keys must still load.
- "Open sessions in" stays.

## 3. Non-goals (v0.3)
- No history or trend charts. The app keeps no history, and adding one is a separate feature.
- No dragging tabs to reorder them, no popping a tab out, no per-workbox windows.
- No new herdr calls for the Dashboard.
- No light theme.

## 4. Anti-goals
- The Dashboard adding load on herdr or on remote workboxes.
- Switching to the Dashboard losing a terminal's scrollback, or detaching it.
- The Home tab being closable, or ⌘W closing the app window.
- A keystroke meant for the terminal triggering Dashboard or list shortcuts (v0.2 §4 still holds).
- Status shown by colour alone.
- Metrics that look precise but aren't. A tile with no data says so ("—", "None").

## 5. Architecture notes
- The terminal store keeps its sessions and tabs. It loses panel placement and panel size, and gains:
  - the active-tab model with the Home tab (`"home"` | terminal tab id);
  - the last active terminal tab;
  - folded groups;
  - the split column (shown and width).
- `TerminalPanel` becomes the workspace: tab strip, the Home tab slot and the terminal views.
- The All agents list and Inspector move from `Home.tsx` into an `AllAgents` view. `Home.tsx` becomes the Home-tab router.
- **New, pure and tested on their own:**
  - Dashboard metric helpers in `lib/` (status counts, oldest blocked, online count, median response over a window, failed count);
  - the tab-order helper (groups in machine order; flat order for ⌘2–⌘9).
- **Rust:**
  - **menu:** a `dashboard` MenuId (⌘1); tab1–tab8 take ⌘2–⌘9; tab9 goes away; the ⌘\ label changes.
  - **settings:** the placement and panel-size fields are replaced by the split-column fields, with old keys tolerated.
